Daedala

A research vessel for the ecology of meaning

Daedala is a speculative instrument. It gathers what research can currently say about how generative AI affects the variety of human expression, and draws the futures that follow. As new studies arrive, its settings change. It shows possible scenarios, not a forecast. The name is Lucretius’s word for the inventive variety of nature.

The living network of meaning, 2026 to 2100

Filaments are the channels through which people make and pass meaning. Their colours are variety; their fine branching is local difference. Turn the globe, change the assumptions, and watch what the network becomes.

Narrowing per pass

By a sixthBy half
Basis

Sourati et al. 2025: when AI rewrote people’s texts, variety of style fell by 16 to 51 per cent. Moon et al. (preprint): in 372,793 admissions essays, variety of words rose while variety of ideas fell.

Erosion of independent capacity

Slow · 2% a yearFast · 10% a year
Basis

Rismanchian et al. 2026: students’ time on AI-solvable problems fell 2.8 per cent a quarter, and retention fell with it. Strömberg, Lei & Wu 2026 (CEPR DP21577): exam scores down a fifth within six months. Liu et al. 2026: worse performance alone after ten minutes of help. The slow end is an assumption.

Confidence

CalibratedInflated
Basis

Shaw & Nave 2026 (working paper): people followed a deliberately wrong AI four times in five, and their confidence rose and did not fall as errors mounted. That calibrated confidence acts as a brake is an assumption.

Concentration

Many systemsA few
Basis

An assumption at both ends. With a few systems, everything routes through the same hubs and settles on one colour. With many systems and inflated confidence, each seals itself off: the strands between them die and the branches loop back on themselves.

Refugia

NoneThree in ten
Basis

The share working under protected conditions, which keeps its colour. In Strömberg et al. about a fifth of users kept working as before and lost little; in Shaw & Nave people overrode a wrong AI one time in five; in Rismanchian et al. the effect vanished under proctoring.

How to read it

Colour is variety: each filament line begins with its own hue, spread in proportion to a region's linguistic diversity. Brightness is activity: web traffic per person online, on a compressed scale. Fine branches are local difference in ideas; as meaning flattens they fade and the trunks thicken. The fuzz is variety of words, which survives: a flattened region stays lush but turns one colour.

What moves it

Each year a region changes by its automated share times its share of people online times the assumed narrowing. As independent capacity erodes, reliance grows and the loss deepens. Confidence decides whether that loop can be broken: when calibrated, noticed loss slows the erosion and lets capacity recover; when inflated, the loss goes unseen, and the network glows brighter as it bleaches. The most linguistically diverse regions are reached later but fall faster. Regions with more automated traffic today meet the automation curve sooner. Concentration speeds the change and decides where the long strands run.

Status

An illustration of an argument, not a measurement. The network is grown at random over a coarse map, and three starting values are real: share of people online (World Bank, from ITU figures), and traffic per person and automated share of traffic (Cloudflare Radar). Linguistic diversity is a stand-in chosen by eye. The settings are tethered to studies but interpreted freely: these are possible scenarios, not projections.

Studies behind the settings

Narrowing: Sourati et al. 2025; Moon et al. (words diversify while ideas converge). Erosion: Rismanchian et al. 2026; Strömberg, Lei & Wu 2026; Liu et al. 2026. Confidence: Shaw & Nave 2026. Refugia: Rismanchian et al.; Strömberg et al.; Shaw & Nave 2026 (people overrode a faulty AI about one time in five). Most are preprints, not yet peer reviewed.
